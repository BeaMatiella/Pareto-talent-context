# Pareto Talent Model - FAQ

**What is Pareto Talent?**
It staffs high-level executive assistants for overwhelmed entrepreneurs. It saves entrepreneurs time by placing trained and managed EAs who work for them full-time. [Interview, ch. 3]

**How is an executive assistant different from a virtual assistant?**
A VA works on tasks or projects for anyone. An EA focuses on one person full-time, on that person's goals, and tries to get as close as possible to cloning them. [Interview, ch. 3]

**How selective is it?**
It takes 1,000+ applicants to produce one Right Hand. The bootcamp is 10 days of interviews and training. In May 2025, 182 EAs had gone through training. [Website; interview]

**Why Latin America?**
Time zone (an EA from Colombia shares Central time), strong English with an accent that works for American ears, useful Spanish for service industries, and cultural alignment (holidays, values). It is not the cheapest place to hire; other regions cost a fraction. [Interview, ch. 6]

**What is the lifetime replacement guarantee?**
If a placement does not work out, Pareto replaces the EA and keeps supporting the client. When it fails, it "makes the client whole", including refunding the placement fee in some cases. [Interview, ch. 12, 16]

**Why do placements fail?**
Mostly mismatch between EA and founder; other causes are founders who never learned to delegate, unclear expectations (grunt work, weekend work), and EAs who quit. Two points of failure: the client and the EA. [Interview, ch. 13, 16]

**How does matching work?**
In the beginning Ivan matched by hand. Later he built a Lovable app connected to Airtable and the OpenAI API that guides Talent Success Managers; it is guidance, not a rule. Interview count is typically one or two candidates; the most a client ever interviewed was four. [Interview, ch. 15, 24]

**How much does it cost?**
See `01_Context/identity-offer-pricing.md`. Current website: $3,600/month, $10,000/quarter or $36,000/year, plus a one-time $3,000 placement fee. [Website]

**How do clients find Pareto?**
Entirely through referrals as of May 2025; referral fee of $1,000 per client that hires. [Interview, ch. 24]

**Would Kasim sell or raise money?**
Never raise; open to a strategic exit. See `01_Context/goals-and-priorities.md`. [Interview, ch. 26]

**Who does Pareto serve inside the company?**
Kasim says the EAs are who they serve, because they are the source of value. Founders pay, but the model dies if Pareto is seen as extracting value from assistants. [Interview, ch. 13]

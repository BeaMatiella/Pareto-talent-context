## Background at a glance
- 15 years of hiring EAs, "dozens of failures" [SOURCE: website scrape]
- Built Solutions 8 to "#1 Google Ads agency worldwide" with nearly 100 employees; 8-figure acquisition [SOURCE: website scrape; day2 homework]
- In the interview: Solutions 8 had almost 100 employees, ~200 clients over time, 40% margins in an industry where 20% is the "high water mark", and 60 unsolicited offers before the sale (sold 2022) [SOURCE: interview]
- Hit his first $1M gross revenue at age 30 (Pareto Talent is his "number six") [SOURCE: interview]

## Who he serves (Pareto Talent)
- Ideal customer: Overwhelmed entrepreneurs and founders who are "doing everything yourself" and want to stop being the bottleneck in their own business [SOURCE: website scrape; interview]. Clients are business owners, from solopreneurs to founders with several companies [SOURCE: interview]
- Customer's main problem: The founder's time goes to busy work; most EA placements fail because no one trains the founder to delegate [SOURCE: website scrape]
- Customer's desired outcome: Focus on "the work only you can do" and on things that move the needle [SOURCE: website scrape; interview]
- Who is NOT a fit: clients who want grunt work, unrealistic expectations, or a personal assistant instead of a business assistant; Pareto has fired a client for this [SOURCE: interview]

## Pricing history (from the May 2025 interview)
- At launch (spring 2024): $2,000 / month + $5,000 one-time placement fee. Described as "a horrible idea, we had no margin", but the first client covered all costs
- At recording (May 2025): "$3,000 a month right now for a placed EA"
- Later, entry-level price was lowered and monthly price raised "in order to afford the service" of managing and supporting clients
- Card payments on the annual package: a $900 fee is added to the client, because merchant fees are otherwise paid out of what would go to assistants and employees
- The website figures above are more recent than the interview. They differ ($3,600/mo and $3,000 fee vs $3,000/mo and $5,000 fee). Treat the website as the current price. [TO FILL: confirm with Kasim]

## Pricing rules and philosophy
- Payment terms / plans: Annual, quarterly or monthly (see above)
- Discounts or guarantees: Lifetime replacement guarantee; when a placement fails, the client is "made whole", and on unsuccessful placements Pareto has refunded the placement fee even when not obliged [SOURCE: interview]
- What is never discounted: "Charge enough money to be extraordinary. There always will be someone cheaper, not always someone better." Low-cost competitors were quoted at about $1,200/month; the price is described as "a great filter" [SOURCE: interview]

## Positioning
- What makes him different:
  - Executive assistants, not virtual assistants: an EA "focuses on one person and one person only, full-time" and gets "as close to cloning that person as they can" [SOURCE: interview]
  - Takes 1,000+ applicants to produce one Right Hand; 10-day bootcamp with interviews [SOURCE: website scrape; interview]
  - Trains the founder as well as the EA; weekly founder community [SOURCE: website scrape]
  - Uses its own product: Kasim has 3 EAs from Pareto; Ivan's EA Ampy was the first internal hire [SOURCE: website scrape]
  - Latin American talent for time zone, English/Spanish, and cultural alignment [SOURCE: interview]
- Main competitors or alternatives: cheap VA agencies (e.g. talent in East Asia at a fraction of the cost), freelancer sites, hiring directly [SOURCE: interview]

  - Website (Sept 2026): "100+ founders are using it", community of "250+ peers" [SOURCE: website scrape]

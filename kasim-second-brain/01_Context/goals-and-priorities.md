## Strategic stance
- Never raise money: the business was profitable from the first client
- Open to an exit, but only to a strategic buyer who could grow it (for example placing 10,000 EAs), not one that would "take it apart"
- Built to exit: systems and SOPs from day one
- Pareto Talent as a "metal detector business": opportunities for new companies come from watching the assistants (AEO agency, techstack.com and others)

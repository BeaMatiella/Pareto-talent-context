## Summary
Kasim and Ivan celebrate $1M in gross revenue for Pareto Talent, a staffing company that places trained executive assistants (mostly from Latin America) with overwhelmed founders. They walk through how Ivan went from Kasim's freelance EA to CTO and co-founder, how a failed self-storage software idea led to the Pareto Talent pivot, how they launched for about $2,100 in costs, and what the business looks like under the hood (team, clients, placements, tools). They discuss premium pricing, their worst days, cancellations and refunds, why matching is the biggest bottleneck, ethics and culture, and their plans for partnerships, exit and scale. They close with advice: stop over-learning, start doing, and land a first paying client.
